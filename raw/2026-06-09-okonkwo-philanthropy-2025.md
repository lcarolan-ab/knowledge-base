selling them **avoided about $96,000 of capital gains**. Gifts of appreciated stock to
public charities and donor-advised funds are deductible up to 30% of AGI, leaving about
$270,000 of headroom in 2025. This is the approach recommended in the March 2026 estate
and liquidity review, and it should be the default for all of the family's giving.
