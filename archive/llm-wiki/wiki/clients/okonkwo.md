# Okonkwo family

Chidi and Amara Okonkwo, net worth about $31.4 million, with $11.2 million (48% of
investable assets) in shares of Larkspur Systems, a fictional public company Chidi
co-founded, at a cost basis of $1.4 million [^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]. The family
also controls the Okonkwo Family Foundation, with assets of $4.8 million
[^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025]. Two deliverables in the library: the March 2026 estate and
liquidity review and the 2025 giving summary [^synthesis].

## Concentration is the organising problem

Every recommendation in both deliverables reduces the Larkspur position: a 10b5-1 plan
selling 10% of the holding a year for four years, a GRAT funded with $3 million of
shares, and a rule that all charitable giving is made in shares rather than cash
[^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]. In 2025 the household gave $110,000 of Larkspur
shares to its donor-advised fund, avoiding about $96,000 of capital gains
[^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025]. See [[topics/concentrated-stock-positions]] and
[[topics/giving-appreciated-shares]].

## Giving

Total family philanthropic activity in 2025 was $412,000: $250,000 of foundation grants
to 23 grantees, $110,000 of DAF grants, and $52,000 of direct cash gifts
[^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025]. Household giving of $162,000 was 18% of AGI, with about
$270,000 of headroom under the 30%-of-AGI limit for gifts of appreciated stock
[^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025]. The foundation's administrative cost of 14% of grants is
roughly double the usual range, driven by 16 grants under $25,000; both deliverables
recommend routing small grants through the DAF [^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025]
[^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]. See [[services/philanthropic-giving-summary]].

## Liquidity and estate

Cash covers about 14 months of $1.6 million annual spending, adequate but eroding at
12% spending growth a year [^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]. The couple has used
$4.2 million of lifetime exemption; the review recommends the GRAT and moving the
$10 million second-to-die policy into an ILIT [^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]. See
[[services/estate-and-liquidity-review]].

## Recommendations still open

File the 10b5-1 plan, fund the GRAT, establish the ILIT
[^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]; set a $250,000 share-giving budget for 2026 and
consolidate the foundation's grantees at the autumn board meeting
[^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025].
