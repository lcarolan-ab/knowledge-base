# Credit card spend analysis

An annual review of every charge on a household's personal cards: statements are
downloaded, merchants are categorised under the firm's spend categorisation standard,
and every charge over $1,000 is reviewed by hand [^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025]
[^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3]. Two are in the library, both for calendar
2025 and both produced under version 2 of the standard
[^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025] [^kessler-card-analysis-2025].

## Who has one

| client | period | total spend | largest category |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[clients/abernathy-ruiz]] | 2025 | $187,400 across three cards [^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025] | travel, 31% [^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025] |
| [[clients/kessler]] | 2025 | $96,300 across two cards [^kessler-card-analysis-2025] | travel, 44% [^kessler-card-analysis-2025] |

## What it reliably finds

- **Unused subscriptions**, in every analysis so far: nine of 47 for the
  Abernathy-Ruiz household ($2,340 a year) and four of 14 for the Kesslers ($860 a
  year) [^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025] [^kessler-card-analysis-2025]. See
  [[topics/unused-subscriptions]].
- **Unredeemed rewards**, valued at 1.5 cents a point: 412,000 points (about $6,200)
  and 180,000 points (about $2,700) respectively [^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025]
  [^kessler-card-analysis-2025] [^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3].
- **Card structure problems**: overlapping annual fees for one household, and a
  travel-heavy household paying 3% foreign transaction fees with no primary travel
  insurance [^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025] [^kessler-card-analysis-2025].
- **Fraud**: two charges totalling $1,140 recovered for the Kesslers
  [^kessler-card-analysis-2025].

## What it does not do

The analysis records charges, not their tax treatment; under version 2 it categorised
charity-related charges as charitable in full, which the giving summary then
re-examined and partly reclassified [^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025]
[^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025]. Version 3 of the standard moves such charges to
a new "events & memberships" category and forbids stating tax treatment in the card
analysis [^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3]. The disagreement between the 2025
deliverables is recorded at [[topics/spend-categorisation]].

## Method changes to watch

Analyses from Q3 2026 use version 3, and charitable and dining totals cannot be
compared across versions without adjustment [^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3].
Both 2025 analyses will need that adjustment when the 2026 editions arrive
[^synthesis]. Their spending totals feed directly into each household's
[[services/cash-flow-projection]].
