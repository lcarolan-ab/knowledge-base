# Philanthropic giving summary

A tax-year review of a household's giving by vehicle, with the deductible amount, the
share of adjusted gross income, the resulting tax position and recommendations on how
to give [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025] [^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025]. Two are in
the library, both for tax year 2025 [^synthesis].

## Who has one

| client | giving | share of AGI | main recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[clients/abernathy-ruiz]] | $23,800 ($22,200 deductible) [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025] | 3.9% [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025] | bunch two years into the DAF in 2026; give RSU shares [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025] |
| [[clients/okonkwo]] | $162,000 by the household, $412,000 including the foundation [^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025] | 18% [^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025] | give only shares; move small grants from the foundation to the DAF [^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025] |

## Recurring advice

Both summaries recommend giving appreciated shares rather than cash, and the Okonkwo
summary shows the effect: $110,000 of shares with a $14,000 basis avoided about
$96,000 of capital gains [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025]
[^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025]. The same advice appears in the Okonkwo estate review,
where it doubles as a way to reduce a concentrated position
[^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]. See [[topics/giving-appreciated-shares]].

Both summaries also use a donor-advised fund as the working vehicle: for bunching two
years of gifts to clear the standard deduction in one case, and for absorbing small
grants that are expensive to make through a private foundation in the other
[^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025] [^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025].

## Where it disagrees with the card analysis

The giving summary decides what is deductible, and it deducts only $2,900 of a $4,500
gala table because $1,600 of goods and services were received, while the card analysis
had categorised the full amount as charitable [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025].
Version 3 of the categorisation standard makes the giving summary the only deliverable
that states tax treatment [^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3]. See
[[topics/spend-categorisation]] and [[services/credit-card-spend-analysis]].
