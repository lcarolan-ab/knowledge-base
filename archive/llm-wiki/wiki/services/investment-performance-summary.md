# Investment performance summary

A quarterly report of portfolio value, allocation, return against a blended benchmark,
and the actions taken or planned [^kessler-performance-2026q2]. One is in the library,
for the Kessler family trust in Q2 2026, so this page is provisional [^synthesis].

## The Kessler Q2 2026 summary

The taxable portfolio stood at $7.6 million at 30 June including $1,000,000 held in
cash for the July lake house closing, and returned +3.8% year to date against +4.6% for
the benchmark; the shortfall is attributed to that cash drag and to an underweight in
international equities, not to security selection [^kessler-performance-2026q2].

Its main value to the library is as a **check on an earlier projection**: the June
liquidation realised $118,000 of gains against the $210,000 the May cash flow revision
estimated, because the highest-basis lots were sold first
[^kessler-performance-2026q2] [^kessler-cash-flow-2026-05]. The $150,000 Roth
conversion the projection recommended was executed in June
[^kessler-performance-2026q2]. See [[topics/funding-a-large-purchase]],
[[services/cash-flow-projection]] and [[clients/kessler]].
