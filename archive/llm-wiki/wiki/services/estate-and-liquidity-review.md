# Estate and liquidity review

A point-in-time review of net worth, concentration, cash runway and estate structure,
ending in an action list [^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]. One is in the library, for
the Okonkwo household in March 2026, so this page is provisional: it describes one
deliverable, not a pattern [^synthesis].

## The Okonkwo review

- **Concentration:** 48% of investable assets in Larkspur Systems shares at a
  $1.4 million basis; a 10b5-1 plan selling 10% a year for four years is recommended,
  with all charitable giving made in shares [^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]. See
  [[topics/concentrated-stock-positions]].
- **Liquidity:** about 14 months of spending in cash, to be held at 12 to 18 months and
  topped up from planned sales [^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026].
- **Estate:** $4.2 million of exemption used; a two-year GRAT funded with $3 million of
  shares and an ILIT for the $10 million second-to-die policy
  [^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026].
- **Foundation:** route grants under $25,000 through the donor-advised fund
  [^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026].

## Follow-through

The giving summary produced three months later confirms the share-giving approach was
already in use in 2025 and quantifies the foundation problem the review pointed at:
administrative cost of 14% of grants, driven by 16 grants under $25,000
[^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025]. See [[services/philanthropic-giving-summary]] and
[[clients/okonkwo]].
