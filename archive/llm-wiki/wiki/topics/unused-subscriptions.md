# Unused subscriptions

Every card analysis in the library has found recurring charges the household no longer
uses, and the finding scales with the number of subscriptions rather than with income
[^synthesis].

| client | recurring charges | unused | annual saving |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[clients/abernathy-ruiz]] | 47 | 9 | $2,340 [^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025] |
| [[clients/kessler]] | 14 | 4 | $860 [^kessler-card-analysis-2025] |

The unused ones are of the same kinds in both households: duplicated streaming
services, memberships and plans that overlap with something else already paid for, and
tools or apps tied to an activity that has stopped [^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025]
[^kessler-card-analysis-2025].

## Definition

Version 3 of the categorisation standard fixes the definitions the 2025 analyses used
informally: a subscription is a charge of the same amount in three or more consecutive
months, and it is unused when there is no correlated activity for 90 days and the
client confirms it on review [^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3]. The annualised
saving from cancellation is a required output
[^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3].

## What is not yet known

Whether the cancellations happen: no follow-up analysis is in the library yet, so the
$2,340 and $860 are recommendations, not realised savings [^synthesis]. See
[[services/credit-card-spend-analysis]] and [[questions/open-questions]].
