# Spend categorisation

How a charge is categorised decides which deliverable's number a client sees, and two
deliverables in the library disagree about the same charges [^synthesis]. The case is
the Abernathy-Ruiz household's 2025 giving: a $4,500 table at the Harbor Arts Gala and
$600 of raffle tickets and merchandise [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025].

## Contradictions

**What is the $4,500 gala table?**

- **The card analysis says charitable, in full.** Under version 2 of the standard any
  charge to a registered charity is charitable, so the analysis reports $9,400 of
  charitable spend including the whole table [^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025].
- **The giving summary says $2,900 of gift and $1,600 of purchase.** The charity's
  acknowledgement values the dinner received at $1,600, which is not deductible, and
  the summary also excludes the $600 of raffle tickets and merchandise the card
  analysis had counted [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025].
- **Version 3 of the standard says neither: it is "events & memberships".** From Q3
  2026 charity-related purchases go to a new category at the full amount, flagged
  `possible-charitable`, and only the giving summary states the deductible portion
  [^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3].

The three positions are not reconciled in the library. The 2025 card analysis was
correct under the rules in force when it was produced and is not restated under
version 3, so the two 2025 numbers will continue to disagree
[^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3]. The client page carries both
[^synthesis]. See [[clients/abernathy-ruiz]].

## Why it matters beyond one table

Version 3 exists because the two teams' figures for the same client disagreed and
clients noticed [^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3]. It also bars comparing
charitable or dining totals across versions without adjustment, which affects every
2026 card analysis that reports a year-on-year change
[^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3]. See
[[services/credit-card-spend-analysis]] and [[services/philanthropic-giving-summary]].
