# Concentrated stock positions

Two households in the library depend on a single company's stock, in different ways:
one holds it, the other is paid in it [^synthesis].

## Holding it: the Okonkwo family

Larkspur Systems shares are $11.2 million, 48% of investable assets, at a $1.4 million
basis, and a 40% fall would remove about $4.5 million of net worth
[^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]. The firm's plan reduces the position through three
channels at once: a 10b5-1 plan selling 10% of the holding a year for four years, a
GRAT funded with $3 million of shares, and all charitable giving made in shares
[^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]. The giving channel is already working: $110,000 of
shares went to the donor-advised fund in 2025, and the 2026 budget is $250,000 of
shares timed with the sale schedule [^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025]. See
[[clients/okonkwo]] and [[topics/giving-appreciated-shares]].

## Being paid in it: the Abernathy-Ruiz household

Dana Abernathy's RSU vesting is $132,000 of $612,000 gross income and stops in March
2028; the cash flow projection shows savings capacity turning negative from 2029 in
its school scenario unless spending adjusts or a new grant is made
[^abernathy-ruiz-cash-flow-2026]. The concentration here is in income rather than
assets, and the projection's remedy is to save while the vesting lasts and revisit at
the cliff [^abernathy-ruiz-cash-flow-2026]. The giving summary's advice to donate
vested shares rather than cash is a small additional way to diversify
[^synthesis]. See [[clients/abernathy-ruiz]] and [[services/cash-flow-projection]].
