# What the library knows

*The compiled view across every deliverable in the library. Start here; follow the
links. All clients, companies and figures are synthetic.*

## The shape of it

Ten deliverables for three client households and one internal standard, compiled into
three client pages, five service pages and five topic pages [^synthesis]. The
deliverable types so far: credit card spend analysis, cash flow projection,
philanthropic giving summary, estate and liquidity review, investment performance
summary, and a methodology memo [^synthesis].

| client | deliverables | the thing to know |
|---|---|---|
| [[clients/abernathy-ruiz]] | card analysis, cash flow, giving summary | savings capacity turns negative from 2029 in the school-plus-RSU-cliff scenario [^abernathy-ruiz-cash-flow-2026] |
| [[clients/kessler]] | card analysis, two cash flow editions, Q2 performance | the lake house took the plan from 2.1% to a 3.5% draw; the earlier verdict is superseded [^kessler-cash-flow-2026-05] |
| [[clients/okonkwo]] | estate review, giving summary | 48% of investable assets in one stock; every recommendation reduces it [^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026] |

## What holds across clients

- **Give shares, not cash.** Recommended for both giving clients and already done by
  one [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025]. See [[topics/giving-appreciated-shares]].
- **Every card analysis finds unused subscriptions.** See
  [[topics/unused-subscriptions]].
- **Projections get superseded, not amended.** The Kessler revision keeps the old
  edition's conclusions in a side-by-side table and withdraws them explicitly
  [^kessler-cash-flow-2026-05]. See [[topics/funding-a-large-purchase]].

## What is contested

One thing: how a charity gala table is categorised. The card analysis, the giving
summary and the new standard give three different answers for the same $4,500, and
the 2025 deliverables will not be restated [^firm-spend-categorisation-standard-v3].
See [[topics/spend-categorisation]].
