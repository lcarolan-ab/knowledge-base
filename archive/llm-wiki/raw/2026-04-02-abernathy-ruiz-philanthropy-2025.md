Only **$2,900 of the $4,500 gala table is deductible**: the charity's acknowledgement

**Note on the card analysis.** The 2025 credit card spend analysis reports $9,400 of
"charitable" spend, categorising the full $4,500 gala table as charitable and
including $600 of raffle tickets and merchandise bought at charity events. This summary
counts $4,300 of direct gifts, because raffle tickets and merchandise are purchases.
The two deliverables describe the same charges under different rules and the
difference is worth settling in the firm's categorisation standard.

## Tax position

Itemised deductions for 2025 were about $34,000 against a standard deduction of about
$31,000, so the giving produced very little incremental tax benefit.

## Recommendations

1. **Bunch two years of giving.** Contribute the planned 2026 and 2027 giving
   (about $40,000) to the donor-advised fund in 2026, itemise in 2026, and take the
   standard deduction in 2027. Grants to charities can continue on the usual schedule.
2. **Give appreciated RSU shares, not cash.** Shares held for more than a year can be
   contributed at fair market value with no capital gains tax on the appreciation.
   Dana's 2024 vest lots qualify from mid-2025.
3. Ask event organisers for the goods-and-services value in writing at purchase, so
   the deductible portion is known before year end.
4. Keep the gala table in the household's giving total for planning purposes, but
   record it separately from gifts.
