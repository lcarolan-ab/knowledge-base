# Estate and liquidity review, March 2026 — Okonkwo household

> **Synthetic demo data.** Invented family, company and figures. Larkspur Systems is a
> fictional company.

**Prepared for:** Chidi and Amara Okonkwo

## Position

| item | value |
|---|---|
| Net worth | $31,400,000 |
| Investable assets | $23,300,000 |
| of which Larkspur Systems shares (Chidi is a co-founder) | **$11,200,000 (48% of investable)**, cost basis $1,400,000 |
| Cash and short-term holdings | $1,900,000 |
| Annual spending | $1,600,000 |
| Okonkwo Family Foundation (separate) | $4,800,000 |

## Concentration

Nearly half of the family's investable assets are in one stock with a very low basis.
A 40% fall in Larkspur would remove about $4.5 million of net worth. Chidi is subject to
trading windows and to Section 16 reporting.

**Recommendation:** adopt a 10b5-1 plan selling **10% of the holding each year for four
years**, which keeps sales inside the company's rules and spreads the gain across tax
years. Use shares, not cash, for all charitable giving: a gift of appreciated stock held
more than a year is deductible at fair market value with no capital gains tax on the
appreciation, so every dollar given in shares reduces concentration at no tax cost.

## Liquidity

Cash and short-term holdings cover about **14 months of spending**. That is adequate,
but the household's spending has grown 12% a year for three years; if that continues
the buffer falls under 12 months by 2027. Hold the buffer at 12 to 18 months and top it
up from the 10b5-1 sales.

## Estate

- The couple has used $4,200,000 of its combined lifetime exemption.
- **GRAT:** fund a two-year grantor retained annuity trust with $3,000,000 of Larkspur
  shares. If the stock outperforms the hurdle rate, the excess passes to the children
  free of gift tax; if it does not, little is lost.
- **ILIT:** the existing $10,000,000 second-to-die policy should be moved into an
  irrevocable life insurance trust so the proceeds sit outside the taxable estate.
- **Foundation grants:** the foundation makes many small grants at a high
  administrative cost. Grants under $25,000 could be routed through the family's
  donor-advised fund instead.

## Actions

1. File the 10b5-1 plan before the next trading window.
2. Fund the GRAT in Q2 2026.
3. Establish the ILIT and transfer the policy.
4. Coordinate 2026 giving with the philanthropy team so it is made in Larkspur shares.
