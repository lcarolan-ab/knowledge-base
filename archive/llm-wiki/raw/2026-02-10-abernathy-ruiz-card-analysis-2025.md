# Credit card spend analysis — Abernathy-Ruiz household, CY2025

> **Synthetic demo data.** The household, the amounts and the card programmes are
> invented for this demonstration. Nothing here describes a real client.

**Prepared for:** Dana Abernathy and Marco Ruiz
**Scope:** all charges on the household's three personal credit cards, 1 Jan – 31 Dec 2025
**Method:** statement download, merchant categorisation under the firm's spend
categorisation standard v2, manual review of every charge over $1,000.

## Headline

Total card spend for 2025 was **$187,400** across three cards, up 9% on 2024. Travel
is the largest category and dining grew fastest.

| category | 2025 spend | share | vs 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Travel | $58,100 | 31% | +6% |
| Household & retail | $41,200 | 22% | +4% |
| Dining | $26,200 | 14% | **+22%** |
| Children & education | $16,900 | 9% | +11% |
| Subscriptions | $11,200 | 6% | +18% |
| Charitable | $9,400 | 5% | +40% |
| Other | $24,400 | 13% | +2% |
| **Total** | **$187,400** | 100% | +9% |

## Findings

1. **Subscriptions.** 47 recurring charges were identified. Nine are unused or
   duplicated (two streaming services with overlapping catalogues, a gym membership
   with no visits since March, three software tools tied to a closed side project,
   and three magazine renewals). Cancelling them saves **$2,340 a year**.
2. **Dining** rose 22% year on year, driven by weekday lunches near Marco's hospital
   and a standing Friday reservation. This is the only category growing faster than
   household income.
3. **Charitable** spend of $9,400 includes a **$4,500 table at the Harbor Arts Gala**
   (October) and $4,900 of other charges to registered charities. Under standard v2
   charity-related charges are categorised as charitable in full.
4. **Rewards.** 412,000 points are unredeemed across the three programmes, worth
   roughly **$6,200** at the firm's default valuation of 1.5 cents per point. Two of the
   programmes devalue points annually.
5. **Card structure.** Three cards with overlapping benefits cost $1,245 in annual
   fees; two would cover every benefit actually used.

## Recommendations

- Consolidate from three cards to two (drop the card whose lounge access duplicates
  another's), saving $550 in annual fees.
- Cancel the nine flagged subscriptions ($2,340 a year).
- Redeem the 412,000 points against 2026 travel before the next devaluation.
- Set a 2026 travel budget of $50,000 and review it in the mid-year check-in.
- Confirm the deductible portion of the gala table with the philanthropy team; the
  card analysis records the charge, not its tax treatment.
