# Kessler family

Ilse (68) and Tomas (72) Kessler, retired, trustees of the Kessler Family Trust, with
investable assets of about $9.5 million at the end of 2025 [^kessler-cash-flow-2025-11].
Four deliverables in the library: a 2025 card analysis, two editions of a ten-year cash
flow projection, and the Q2 2026 performance summary [^synthesis].

## The lake house

In March 2026 the trustees decided to buy a lake house for $1,900,000, closing in July
2026, which invalidated the November 2025 projection's assumption of no major purchases
[^kessler-cash-flow-2026-05]. The firm's advice was to liquidate $1,000,000 from the
taxable portfolio and borrow $900,000 on a pledged-asset line rather than sell
$1,900,000 of appreciated holdings [^kessler-cash-flow-2026-05]. The June sale realised
$118,000 of gains against the $210,000 estimated, because the highest-basis lots were
sold first [^kessler-performance-2026q2]. See [[topics/funding-a-large-purchase]].

## Sustainability, then and now

| | November 2025 edition | May 2026 revision |
|---|---|---|
| annual spending | $260,000 [^kessler-cash-flow-2025-11] | $298,000 [^kessler-cash-flow-2026-05] |
| net portfolio draw | 2.1% [^kessler-cash-flow-2025-11] | 3.5%, at the ceiling [^kessler-cash-flow-2026-05] |
| verdict | sustainable indefinitely [^kessler-cash-flow-2025-11] | sustainable only with the liquidate-and-borrow structure [^kessler-cash-flow-2026-05] |

**Superseded claim (2026-05-20).** The November 2025 edition concluded the plan was
sustainable indefinitely with $70,000 a year of spending headroom
[^kessler-cash-flow-2025-11]. The May 2026 revision withdraws both conclusions: the
headroom is gone and the draw sits at the 3.5% ceiling [^kessler-cash-flow-2026-05].
The earlier edition is kept in the library as the record of what was advised at the
time [^synthesis]. See [[services/cash-flow-projection]].

## Spending and cards

2025 card spend was $96,300, 44% of it travel, dominated by a $28,100 six-week trip to
Europe paid on a card with a 3% foreign transaction fee and no primary travel insurance
[^kessler-card-analysis-2025]. Four of 14 subscriptions were unused ($860 a year) and
two fraudulent charges totalling $1,140 were recovered [^kessler-card-analysis-2025].
See [[services/credit-card-spend-analysis]] and [[topics/unused-subscriptions]].

## Investments

The taxable portfolio stood at $7.6 million at 30 June 2026 including the $1,000,000
held for closing, returning +3.8% year to date against +4.6% for the benchmark; the gap
is cash drag ahead of closing and an underweight to international equities
[^kessler-performance-2026q2]. A $150,000 Roth conversion was executed in June, the
first of two planned before RMDs begin in 2027 [^kessler-performance-2026q2]
[^kessler-cash-flow-2026-05]. See [[services/investment-performance-summary]].
