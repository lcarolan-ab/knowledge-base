# Abernathy-Ruiz household

Dana Abernathy and Marco Ruiz, two children aged 9 and 12 in 2026, combined gross income
of about $612,000 of which $132,000 is RSU vesting that ends in March 2028
[^abernathy-ruiz-cash-flow-2026]. Three deliverables in the library: a 2025 credit card
spend analysis, a 2026–2030 cash flow projection, and a 2025 giving summary [^synthesis].

## What the firm has told them

**Spending.** 2025 card spend was $187,400 across three cards, up 9% on 2024, with
travel the largest category (31%) and dining growing fastest (+22%)
[^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025]. Nine of 47 subscriptions were unused, worth
$2,340 a year, and 412,000 points (about $6,200) sat unredeemed
[^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025]. See [[services/credit-card-spend-analysis]] and
[[topics/unused-subscriptions]].

**Cash flow.** Savings capacity is about $107,600 a year in the base case, falling to
about $43,600 if both children move to independent school from 2027, and turning
negative from 2029 once the RSU grant has fully vested unless spending adjusts
[^abernathy-ruiz-cash-flow-2026]. The projection carries card spend forward flat at the
2025 level and names that as its most fragile assumption
[^abernathy-ruiz-cash-flow-2026]. See [[services/cash-flow-projection]].

**Giving.** 2025 giving was $23,800, or 3.9% of AGI, of which $22,200 was deductible,
and produced almost no tax benefit because itemised deductions barely exceeded the
standard deduction [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025]. The recommendation is to bunch
2026 and 2027 giving into the donor-advised fund in 2026 and to give appreciated RSU
shares rather than cash [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025]. See
[[services/philanthropic-giving-summary]] and [[topics/giving-appreciated-shares]].

## Open items across deliverables

- The card analysis and the giving summary categorise the same $4,500 gala table
  differently; the disagreement is recorded at [[topics/spend-categorisation]]
  [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025].
- The cash flow's flat-spend assumption has not yet been checked against 2026 card
  data [^synthesis]. See [[questions/open-questions]].
- The household's income depends heavily on a single equity grant until 2028
  [^abernathy-ruiz-cash-flow-2026]. See [[topics/concentrated-stock-positions]].

## Recommendations still open

Consolidate to two cards, cancel the nine subscriptions, redeem points against 2026
travel [^abernathy-ruiz-card-analysis-2025]; fund 401(k)s and 529s and hold six months
of spend in cash [^abernathy-ruiz-cash-flow-2026]; bunch giving in 2026
[^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025].
