# Giving appreciated shares

The firm's standing advice, repeated in three deliverables: give shares held for more
than a year rather than cash, because they are deductible at fair market value and the
appreciation is never taxed [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025]
[^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025] [^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026].

## Evidence in the library

- **Okonkwo, 2025:** $110,000 of Larkspur shares with a $14,000 basis contributed to
  a donor-advised fund, avoiding about $96,000 of capital gains, with about $270,000
  of headroom left under the 30%-of-AGI limit [^okonkwo-philanthropy-2025].
- **Abernathy-Ruiz, recommended for 2026:** contribute 2024-vest RSU lots, which
  qualify as long-term from mid-2025, instead of the cash used in 2025
  [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025].

## Two jobs at once

For a concentrated holder the gift is also a diversification tool, since every dollar
given in shares reduces the position at no tax cost; the Okonkwo estate review makes
share-giving a rule for that reason [^okonkwo-estate-liquidity-2026]. For a household
whose giving barely clears the standard deduction, shares plus bunching two years of
gifts into a donor-advised fund in one year is what turns the giving into a deduction
at all [^abernathy-ruiz-philanthropy-2025]. See [[topics/concentrated-stock-positions]],
[[services/philanthropic-giving-summary]], [[clients/okonkwo]] and
[[clients/abernathy-ruiz]].
