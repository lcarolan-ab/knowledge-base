Built for a 23-year-old starting a first job at $78,000 with one student card and a
thin credit file. Recommends a two-card setup (a no-fee cash-back card for everyday
spend and a starter travel card once the file is six months older), a payment
automation rule so utilisation stays under 10%, and a $3,000 starter emergency fund
before any rewards optimisation. Includes a one-page "first year" checklist the client
can follow without us.

## Outline

- Where a new graduate's credit file starts, and what moves it
- The two-card setup: which cards and why
- Utilisation, autopay and the 10% rule
- Rewards: what is worth chasing at this spend level, and what is not
- The starter emergency fund comes first
- First-year checklist
- Appendix: card comparison table
