Companion to the new-graduate card analysis for the same client. Models a $78,000
starting salary, $41,000 of federal student loans, and rent in a high-cost city over
five years. Compares standard repayment against an income-driven plan, shows that the
employer 401(k) match beats extra loan payments in every scenario, and sets a savings
order: match, emergency fund, then loans above 6%.

## Outline

- Starting position: salary, loans, rent
- Base case: where the money goes in year one
- Standard versus income-driven repayment
- The 401(k) match comes before extra loan payments
- Savings order and the five-year picture
- What changes with a raise, a move, or a partner
