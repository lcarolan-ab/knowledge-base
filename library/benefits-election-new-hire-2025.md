A reusable guide we send to clients starting a first job: how to read the benefits
enrolment form, what to elect in the 401(k) (at least the match; Roth if in a low
bracket), the HSA when a high-deductible plan is offered, and which employer insurance
to take versus decline. Written for someone with no prior benefits experience.

## Outline

- Reading the enrolment form
- 401(k): the match, Roth versus traditional, the default fund
- Health plan choice and the HSA
- Employer life and disability cover: take, top up, or decline
- Things to do in the first 30 days
