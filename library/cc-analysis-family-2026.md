Full-year review of $187,000 of card spend across three cards for a two-earner household
with two children. Found nine unused subscriptions ($2,340 a year), 412,000 unredeemed
points, and dining growing 22% year on year. Recommends consolidating to two cards,
redeeming points before the next devaluation, and a travel budget. The categorisation
follows version 2 of the firm's standard; the charitable line needs the giving team's
treatment of a gala table.

## Outline

- Headline: $187,400 across three cards, up 9%
- Category breakdown and year-on-year movement
- Subscriptions: 47 recurring charges, 9 unused
- Dining is the fastest-growing category
- Rewards: 412,000 points and two devaluation dates
- Card structure: three cards, two needed
- Recommendations and savings
- Appendix: method and categorisation notes
