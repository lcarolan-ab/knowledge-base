Found the household under-insured on term life (coverage at 4x income against a need of
about 12x while the children are young) and with no own-occupation disability cover for
the higher earner. Recommends a 20-year level term policy, an own-occupation disability
policy, and raising the umbrella limit. Reviews beneficiary designations against the
estate documents.

## Outline

- Coverage inventory
- Term life: the gap and the 20-year recommendation
- Disability: own-occupation cover for the higher earner
- Umbrella liability
- Beneficiary designations and the estate plan
