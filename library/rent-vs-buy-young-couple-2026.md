Interactive model for a couple in their late twenties with $120,000 saved, comparing
renting against buying a $650,000 condo over seven years. Buying wins only if they stay
past year five and rents rise more than 3% a year; the down payment would also drain the
emergency fund, which is the main argument for waiting eighteen months.

## Outline

- Inputs: savings, income, rent, target price
- Seven-year comparison under three rent-growth cases
- Break-even year
- The emergency fund problem
- Recommendation and what would change it
