Revision of a November 2025 projection after the clients decided to buy a $1.9 million
lake house. Shows the net portfolio draw rising from 2.1% to 3.5%, at the firm's
sustainability ceiling, and recommends liquidating $1 million of high-basis lots and
borrowing $900,000 on a pledged-asset line rather than selling appreciated holdings.
Includes a Roth conversion window before required distributions begin.

## Outline

- What changed since November
- Funding the purchase: sell, borrow, or both
- Net draw and the 3.5% ceiling
- Roth conversions in the low-bracket window
- Ten-year projection under the recommended structure
- Risks: a second property, a spending increase, a market fall
