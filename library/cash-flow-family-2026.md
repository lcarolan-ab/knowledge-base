Projection for a household with $612,000 of income, $132,000 of it RSU vesting that ends
in 2028. Base-case savings capacity of about $108,000 a year falls to $44,000 if both
children move to independent school, and turns negative from 2029 after the vesting
cliff unless spending adjusts. Recommends funding 401(k)s and 529s while the vesting
lasts and holding six months of spend in cash.

## Outline

- Income, tax and spend in 2026
- The flat-spend assumption and why it is fragile
- Scenario: independent school from 2027
- Scenario: the RSU cliff in 2028
- Savings priorities while vesting lasts
- Review triggers
