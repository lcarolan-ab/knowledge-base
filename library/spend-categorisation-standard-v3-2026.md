The rules every credit card analysis follows from Q3 2026: category definitions, the
new "events and memberships" category for charity galas and auction purchases, the
90-day definition of an unused subscription, and the 1.5-cents-per-point default for
rewards. Analyses produced under version 2 are not restated.

## Outline

- Why version 3 exists
- Category definitions
- Charity-related purchases and the giving team's role
- Subscriptions: the 90-day rule
- Rewards valuation
- Comparing across versions
