Tax-year summary of $23,800 of giving that produced almost no tax benefit because
itemised deductions barely cleared the standard deduction. Recommends bunching two years
of giving into the donor-advised fund in one year and contributing appreciated RSU
shares rather than cash. Works through the deductible portion of a charity gala table.

## Outline

- Giving by vehicle and what was deductible
- Why the tax benefit was small
- Bunching: two years into the DAF in one
- Give appreciated shares, not cash
- The gala table and the goods-and-services rule
