For a resident with $310,000 of federal loans moving to an attending salary of $340,000.
Compares Public Service Loan Forgiveness at a non-profit hospital against refinancing at
a private practice, with the income jump modelled year by year. The forgiveness path wins
by about $95,000 if the client stays in qualifying employment for the remaining five
years; refinancing wins if they leave within two.

## Outline

- Loan inventory and the residency-to-attending income jump
- Path A: PSLF at a qualifying employer
- Path B: refinance and pay aggressively
- Break-even on years of qualifying employment
- Tax filing status and the income-driven payment
- Decision checklist
