For a household with 48% of investable assets in one low-basis stock. Recommends a
10b5-1 plan selling 10% of the holding a year, a two-year GRAT funded with shares, moving
a $10 million second-to-die policy into an ILIT, and making all charitable gifts in
shares. Liquidity buffer of 14 months is adequate but eroding at the current spending
growth rate.

## Outline

- Net worth and the concentration problem
- 10b5-1 plan: schedule and trading windows
- GRAT: mechanics and what happens if the stock underperforms
- ILIT for the existing policy
- Liquidity: the 12-to-18-month rule
- Charitable giving as diversification
- Action list with dates
