Review of $96,000 of annual card spend for a retired couple, 44% of it travel including
a six-week trip paid on a card with a 3% foreign transaction fee and no travel
insurance. Recommends moving travel to a no-fee card with primary travel cover,
cancelling four unused subscriptions, submitting $2,200 of unclaimed medical receipts,
and redeeming 180,000 points. Two fraudulent charges were caught and recovered.
