Explains RSUs, ISOs and the ESPP for a 28-year-old engineer whose company stock is now
35% of net worth. Covers what is taxed when, why RSUs are taxed like salary at vest, the
AMT trap on ISOs, and a sell-at-vest default with a written exception process. Reusable
as a template for other tech-employee clients.

## Outline

- Your grants: RSUs, ISOs, ESPP
- What is taxed, and when
- The AMT trap on ISO exercises
- Sell at vest as the default
- When to hold, and how to write it down
- Concentration: a 35% position in one stock
- Template notes for reuse
